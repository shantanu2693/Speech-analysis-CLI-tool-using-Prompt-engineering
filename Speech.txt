Speaker: H.E. Amina Mohammed, Deputy Secretary-General of the United NationsEvent: Global Sustainable Development ForumLocation: Geneva, SwitzerlandTranscript:"Excellencies, distinguished delegates, and partners,We stand at a critical juncture in human history. The challenges we face today—from the escalating climate crisis to persistent global inequalities—do not respect national borders. They are borderless threats that demand borderless solutions.When we look back at the commitments we have made, it is clear that our greatest strength lies not in isolation, but in solidarity. We cannot build a prosperous and secure future for some while leaving the marginalized behind.Every policy debated, every resource allocated, and every partnership forged is a step toward fulfilling our shared promise. We possess the knowledge, the technology, and the resources to eradicate poverty and protect our fragile planet. What we need now is the unwavering political will to translate these ideals into reality.Let us move forward with the courage to collaborate, the wisdom to innovate, and the compassion to uplift those most in need. Our shared destiny is what we choose to make it, together.Thank you."
